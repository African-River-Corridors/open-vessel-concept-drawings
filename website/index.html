Open Vessel Concept Design

An open instrument for the concept design of inland and coastal vessels.

Give it a hull and a cargo. It gives you capacity, draught, weights, tank arrangement, stability, resistance and power, each result with its rule, its source and its checks. Free to use, open formats, no gatekeeping.

What it is, and what it is not

✓ It is

  • A concept and preliminary design tool for inland and coastal vessels and tug-barge systems.
  • A calculation core you can read: every result names its rule set, edition and paragraph, and every coefficient its source.
  • Calibrated against real barges and published design points, with the cases in the open.
  • A desktop app, a command line and a Python library over one Rust core.
  • Drawings to an open Drawing Format Standard, as SVG, PDF and DXF.

✕ It is not

  • Class approval, a stability booklet or construction approval. It never will be.
  • A substitute for a naval architect, a yard design office or a class surveyor.
  • A tool for seagoing ships under the IACS Common Structural Rules (not before version 1.0).
  • Verified yet. Known engineering findings are under review in the open.

Made in the tradition of the drawing office and the yard

Before the screen, a vessel was drawn in the drawing office and built from the drawings. Draughtsmen laid down the lines, then drew the construction: a section at each key station, every plate, frame and girder named on a leader line, the title lettered by hand and underlined. The drawings went out as blueprints, white line on blue, to the people in the yard who built from them.

That work took skill on both sides of the yard door: the judgement to arrange a structure that is strong and simple to build, and the craft to build it true to the drawing. It still does.

OVCD is made in that spirit. It does the arithmetic quickly and shows its working. The judgement stays with you. It is one more instrument in the drawing office, not a machine that replaces the people who draw and build.

  1. Lines planSheer, half-breadth and body plan: the hull in three views.
  2. Table of offsetsThe hull as numbers. In OVCD, the hull is a sections table too.
  3. Construction sectionEach part named on a leader. Name the part, state the fact: the voice of OVCD's text.
  4. Title blockWhat it is, at what scale, by whom. Every OVCD result says what it is and which rule it used.

Install

Web app

OVCD runs in your browser. Nothing to install. The calculation core and the drawings run on your computer, so your designs stay there: no server sees them.

Open in your browser

The same concept design tool: results are not verified. Open and save files as on the desktop; the browser keeps your last Design.

Desktop app

Take the installer for your computer from the newest release. It needs no Python.

  • Windows: OVCD_<version>_x64-setup.exe
  • macOS, Apple silicon: OVCD_<version>_aarch64.dmg
  • macOS, Intel: OVCD_<version>_x64.dmg

The installers are not signed yet. Your system warns you once. Check the download with SHA256SUMS.txt.

Python library

Python 3.12 or later.

pip install git+https://github.com/African-River-Corridors/open-vessel-concept-drawings

From source

You need Git, Node 24 and Rust.

git clone https://github.com/African-River-Corridors/open-vessel-concept-drawings
cd open-vessel-concept-drawings/app
npm ci
npx tauri dev

Open source, and open to practitioners

OVCD is Apache 2.0 licensed. The methods are documented, the formats are open and the calibration cases are public. Nothing sits behind a login.

The project needs people who know ships. Expert contributors are welcome as reviewers: an evaluation harness sorts most problems by machine, so your time goes to the cases only a person can judge. Naval architects, yard engineers, surveyors and students are all welcome.

How to contribute Open issues

How it is made

We use AI assistants to help write code and documents, and we say so openly. Every change is tested, reviewed and recorded in the public history. The engineering judgement, and the responsibility, stay with people.

Responsible use

OVCD is for concept and preliminary design. Its results are not verified and there is no warranty. Do not use it to approve, build or operate a vessel. A real design needs a qualified naval architect, the applicable class rules and the approval of the flag state or classification society.

Every result states its rule set and its calibrated range. Outside that range, treat a result as a question, not an answer.

1 What OVCD does today

  • Geometry: horizontal cylinders with hemispherical or 2:1 ellipsoidal heads, sized from the hull. The outer diameter includes the insulation.
  • Wall: the pressure-vessel formula (ASME UG-27 form) on the steel diameter, with IGC minimum thickness and E = 0.95 (since #292).
  • Arrangement: protective distance d(Vc) per tank, with the dome in Vc (IGC 2.4.1.1, 2.4.2); L/D ≤ 7.
  • Weight: shell plate × tank_structure_factor (1.30), plus insulation.
  • Drawing: in section only: the shell, a dome curve and a 120° cradle.
Two defects found during this research (not fixed yet):
  • fix 2:1 head area is 27 % low. tanks.rs uses 1.084·π·R² (3.405 R²) per head for the steel and the insulation. The exact half-area of a 2:1 oblate spheroid is 4.336 R² = 1.084·D².
  • fix S690QL1 allowable stress is 285 MPa (Rm/2.7, from an inland design). IGC 4.23.3.1 gives f = min(Rm/3, Re/1.5) = min(770/3, 690/1.5) = 256.7 MPa for C-Mn steel at sea.

How the codes fit together

The IGC sets the criteria: minimum pressure, allowable stress, load cases. For the actual sizing of shells, heads and saddles it points to “accepted pressure vessel design theory” and “a recognized standard” (4.23.2.4, 4.23.2.5.2). The class societies agree a common reading through IACS. The IGF repeats the IGC for fuel tanks, with stronger collision loads on small ships. ADN adds inland arrangement limits.

3 From code to Rule

Each Rule follows spec 0004: an id <family>/<paragraph>/<slug>, a kind, a Measure, a comparison and an outcome. “Engine” says whether OVCD can evaluate it now. When it cannot, the Rule is still listed, with evaluated: false and a gap. Proposed Measure ids are marked new.

3.3 Rule-set placement

  • igc-2g: every igc/* and iacs/* Rule above.
  • adn-g or the inland stack: the adn/* Rules, plus the ADN opening and relief-valve heights (9.3.1.22.1(b), 9.3.1.22.3).
  • A future igf set: the same Type C Rules with the IGF collision table, when OVCD sizes fuel tanks.

4 How we draw the tank

The drawing follows the parts a published Type C support patent draws and names (US 8,245,658 Figs 5A, 5B), with proportions from published tanks. The sheet stays black on white, as the Drawing Format Standard prints it. These are schematic: OVCD would draw them to scale from the design.

5 Weight and bill of quantities

The Rules and the drawing feed a component weight model that replaces the single 1.30 factor. Published marine LNG tanks weigh 0.096–0.115 t per m³ (240 t for a 2,500 m³ bilobe; 155 t for a 1,350 m³ tank). A rough hand check with OVCD’s formulas gives about 0.05 t/m³, so the parts below need calibrating before any default changes.

7 Open items

  • Saddle stresses: Zick 1951 and PD 5500 Annex G are paywalled, so ring sizing and saddle position stay inputs.
  • Shell and head formulas: ASME VIII-1 and EN 13445-3 are not held, so the wall Measure stays unverified.
  • External pressure / buckling: no free method held.
  • Dome rule: based on only two tanks; no dome height for tanks under 1,000 m³.
  • Weight: no published breakdown by part for any marine tank.
  • Insulation: no installed PUF density published.
  • Class rules: DNV and LR gas rules not read (sign-up needed).
  • To check: IACS UR G1 App. 1 prints its scope as “L < 50 m”, likely a misprint. IGF amendments after MSC 108 not checked.

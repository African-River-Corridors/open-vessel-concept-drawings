- Resistance (issue #735, ADR 0024): a fourth `resistance_method`, `barge_form_factor`: ITTC-1957 friction times (1 + k1 + dk + w), with the Holtrop 1984 form factor, the Millward (1989) shallow-water increase dk = 0.644 (T/h)^1.72 and the Froude-number rise of the bluff-barge fit. Checked against 22 barge towing-tank points (`calibration/resistance_cases.csv`, `calibration/resistance_results.md`): mean absolute error 17 %, against 26 % for Holtrop-Mennen and 32 % for the barge fit, which reads low on every point. Its declared range is that of the data (Fn up to 0.137, Cp 0.768 to 0.877, L/B 7.59 to 16.29, B/T 4.05 to 8.12); Rule `ovd/resistance/method-in-range` warns outside it. `barge_fit` stays the default, so no saved or new Design moves.

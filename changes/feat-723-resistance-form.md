- Resistance (issue #723, ADR 0023): a new input `resistance_method`. `barge_fit` is today's bluff-barge fit and stays the default, so no saved or new Design moves. `holtrop_mennen` uses Holtrop 1984 (with the 1982 bulb, transom and correlation terms) at any fullness; `by_block_coefficient` uses it up to Cp 0.85 and the barge fit above. A Design that chooses a method gets a warning check per environment, `resistance method in range`, naming the method and each of Fn, Cp, L/B and B/T outside its declared range (Ortigosa et al. 2009, Table 1); Rule `ovd/resistance/method-in-range` in Rule Set `ovd` 3.1.0 reports it. Verified against the worked example of Holtrop and Mennen 1982 (total 1,792.1 kN against 1,793.3 kN).
- Example `gas-140x29-coastal-lng`: a Cb sweep (Options N1 to N9, Cb 0.97 to 0.74) at a fixed installed power of 1,729 kW, with `by_block_coefficient`.
